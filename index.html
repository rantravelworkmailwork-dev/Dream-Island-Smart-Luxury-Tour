Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive in Sri Lanka and be warmly welcomed by your chauffeur at the airport. Transfer by private air-conditioned vehicle to your hotel in the Habarana / Sigiriya region. Check in and spend the rest of the day relaxing at the hotel after your journey.

🤝 Airport Meet & Greet 🚗 Private Transfer 🏨 Hotel Relaxation
Overnight: Habarana / Sigiriya — choice of Habarana Village by Cinnamon, Amaya Lake or Kassapa Lion Rock
Meals: Dinner  |  Night 1 of 3

MorningClimb the iconic Sigiriya Rock Fortress to enjoy sweeping panoramic views across the surrounding plains and explore ancient rock frescoes.

AfternoonExperience authentic rural life on an immersive Village Tour — a gentle glimpse into traditional Sri Lankan farming, cooking, and daily village living.

🥾 Sigiriya 🌾 Village Tour
Overnight: Habarana / Sigiriya — choice of Habarana Village by Cinnamon, Amaya Lake or Kassapa Lion Rock
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner  |  Night 2 of 3

MorningEnjoy a relaxed morning and breakfast at your hotel.

AfternoonEmbark on an exciting jeep safari in Habarana / Minneriya in search of wild elephants and native wildlife in their natural habitat.

EveningUnwind and revitalize your mind and body with a traditional Sri Lankan Ayurveda massage before dinner at the hotel.

🐘 Habarana Safari 🌿 Ayurveda Massage
Overnight: Habarana / Sigiriya — choice of Habarana Village by Cinnamon, Amaya Lake or Kassapa Lion Rock
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner  |  Night 3 of 3

After breakfast, check out and journey towards Kandy. En route, visit the historic Golden Temple / Dambulla Cave Temple and stop at a Spice Garden to learn about indigenous herbs and spices. Upon arrival in Kandy, pay homage at the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, followed by an evening Sri Lankan traditional cultural dance show.

🛕 Golden Temple / Cave Temple 🌶️ Spice Garden 🛕 Temple of Tooth Relic 💃 Cultural Show
Overnight: Kandy — choice of Golden Crown, Amaya Boutique Hill or Thilanka Kandy
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Start the morning with a visit to a famous Gem Museum in Kandy. Afterwards, ascend into the cool hill country, visiting a working Tea Garden & Tea Factory to discover the art of Ceylon tea-making. Stop at the breathtaking Ramboda Falls before reaching Nuwara Eliya for check-in.

💎 Gem Museum ☕ Tea Garden & Factory 🌊 Ramboda Falls
Overnight: Nuwara Eliya — choice of Galway Heights, Araliya Green Hills or Lynden Grove
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Enjoy breakfast in the misty hills before descending towards Colombo, taking in stunning mountain and valley viewpoints along the way. Upon arriving in Colombo, take a 1.5-hour city tour highlighting landmark architecture, shopping districts, and coastal promenades before checking into your hotel.

🏞️ Scenic Viewpoints 🏙️ 1.5-Hour Colombo City Tour
Overnight: Colombo / Near Airport — choice of Marino Beach Colombo, Kingsbury Colombo or Grandbell Hotel
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, check out and transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your flight home, bringing your memorable Sri Lankan journey to a close.

Departure Transfer: Private transfer to the airport.
Meals: Breakfast
