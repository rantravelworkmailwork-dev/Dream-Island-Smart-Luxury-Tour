Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive in Sri Lanka and be welcomed by your chauffeur at the airport. Transfer by private air-conditioned vehicle to your hotel in the Habarana / Sigiriya region for check-in and a restful first night.

🤝 Airport Meet & Greet 🚗 Private Transfer
Overnight: Habarana / Sigiriya — choice of Habarana Village by Cinnamon, Amaya Lake or Kassapa Lion Rock
Meals: Dinner  |  Night 1 of 3

MorningClimb Pidurangala Rock for sweeping views across the plains, with Sigiriya Rock Fortress rising opposite. Return to the hotel for breakfast.

AfternoonExperience authentic rural life on a Village Tour — a gentle glimpse into traditional Sri Lankan farming and daily living.

🥾 Pidurangala 🌾 Village Tour
Overnight: Habarana / Sigiriya — choice of Habarana Village by Cinnamon, Amaya Lake or Kassapa Lion Rock
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner  |  Night 2 of 3

MorningBreakfast at the hotel, then time to relax at your leisure.

AfternoonHead out on a jeep safari in Habarana in search of wild elephants and other wildlife.

EveningUnwind with a traditional Ayurveda massage, followed by dinner at the hotel.

🐘 Habarana Safari 🌿 Ayurveda Massage
Overnight: Habarana / Sigiriya — choice of Habarana Village by Cinnamon, Amaya Lake or Kassapa Lion Rock
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner  |  Night 3 of 3

After breakfast, check out and drive to Kandy. On the way, visit the Golden Temple / Cave Temple of Dambulla and stop at a Spice Garden to learn about Sri Lanka's aromatic herbs and spices. In the evening, enjoy a Sri Lankan traditional cultural show in Kandy.

🛕 Golden Temple / Cave Temple 🌶️ Spice Garden 💃 Cultural Show
Overnight: Kandy — choice of Golden Crown, Amaya Boutique Hill or Thilanka Kandy
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, stroll through the Botanical Garden before climbing into the hill country. Pause at Ramboda Falls, wind through emerald tea plantations, and tour a working Tea Garden & Factory before arriving in Nuwara Eliya.

🛕 Kandy Temple of the Tooth Relic 🌊 Ramboda Falls ☕ Tea Garden & Factory
Overnight: Nuwara Eliya — choice of Galway Heights, Araliya Green Hills or Lynden Grove
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

Enjoy a final breakfast in the hills, then descend by scenic mountain road towards Colombo. Check in to your hotel near the airport and relax for the evening.

Overnight: Colombo / Near Airport — choice of Marino Beach Colombo, Kingsbury Colombo or Grandbell Hotel
Meals: Breakfast & Dinner

After breakfast, check out and transfer to the airport for your departure flight, carrying home the memories of Sri Lanka.

Departure Transfer: Private transfer to the airport.
